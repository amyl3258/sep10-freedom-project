## Choosing my topic

For this project, I decided to focus on medical practice because of the impact its technology had on healthcare. Initially, I considered exploring either healthcare or animations as my topic, but the topic that stood out to me the most was medical practice. I had a huge interest in medical field, and that interest grew when I learned about how medicine was an important part of society, with doctors new inventions were able to help provide better care for their patients, how it helps them identity diseases, and especially how it creates a big impact in performing complicated procedures. The articles that helped spark my growth in interest were [Application of digital technology in medical practice](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/public-health/articles/10.3389/fpubh.2026.1722648/full) by Frontiers, [Integrating Technology into Clinical Practice](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-031-88117-6_15) by Springer Nature, and [Transformative Frontiers: A Comprehensive Review of Emerging Technologies in Modern Healthcare](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11027446/) by PubMed Central. These articles introduced me to different ways technology could be used, especially in medicine where these innovations made me even more interested in exploring the relationship between technology and medical practice.

## Engineering Design Process

At this point, I'm working on understanding and defining the problem. I want to address the **_lack of awareness people have of technology and how it is used in medical practice_**. Many technologies today changed the way doctors diagnose diseases, treat patients, and perform surgeries, but not everyone understands how these technologies work or why they are important. The next stage of the EDP process is researching the problem. I hope to use articles to understand how technologies and their impact, while discussing the topic of technology with the people around me to gain a deeper understanding on what they already know about medical technology.

#### Researching 

Researching on Google is an important skill I developed when working on the project. When I started looking into the project about technology in medical practice, I needed to find articles that talked about how technology was being used. When I just googled "Medical Practice" none of the articles talked about technology. This led me to become more specific so that I was able to get the result I wanted, which was information on how technology impacted medical practices today. Searching for this topic helped me find useful information which sparked my interest as I skimmed through the titles of articles that seemed the most compelling, like [Application of digital technology in medical practice](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/public-health/articles/10.3389/fpubh.2026.1722648/full) and [Integrating Technology into Clinical Practice](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-031-88117-6_15). As I researched, it allowed me to find reliable information by filtering the results down to the topics I search, learning unfamiliar topics, and making informed decisions.

#### Critical Thinking

Another skill I developed was critical thinking. While researching the project, I realized how it was important to examine information carefully and to consider if the information provided is reliable. When reading the articles about medical innovations, I needed to research about both the advantages and disadvantages instead of just the positive aspects. I couldn't just focus on how the benefits these medical technologies had with helping doctors treat patients, but the limitations and challenges that came with it. When I looked a the first article, [Application of digital technology in medical practice](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/public-health/articles/10.3389/fpubh.2026.1722648/full), there weren't just positive aspects to using technology in medicine, there were challenges listed down. This led me to think more deeply about the information I was give, evaluating the information, comparing different viewpoints, and questioning evidence.

## Next Steps

I look forward to learning more about the different technologies used in medical practice, their impact, and the challenges and limitations that come with using these technologies. I hope to gain a better understanding about this topic so I can expand my knowledge on medical technology.
