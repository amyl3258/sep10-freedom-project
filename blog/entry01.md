## Choosing my topic

For this project, I decided to focus on medical practice because of the impact its technology had on healthcare. Initially, I considered exploring either healthcare or animations as my topic, but the topic that stood out to me the most was medical practice. I had a huge interest in medical field, and that interest grew when I learned about how medicine was an important part of society, with doctors new inventions were able to help provide better care for their patients, how it helps them identity diseases, and especially how it creates a big impact in performing complicated diseases. The articles that helped spark my growth in interest were [Application of digital technology in medical practice](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/public-health/articles/10.3389/fpubh.2026.1722648/full) by Frontiers, [Integrating Technology into Clinical Practice](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-031-88117-6_15) by Springer Nature, and [Transformative Frontiers: A Comprehensive Review of Emerging Technologies in Modern Healthcare](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11027446/) by PubMed Central.
