## Content

As I researched my topic, Medical Practice, I found software and hardware technologies that were used in this concept. At first, I searched up _"Medical Practice Technologies"_ which didn't give me the result I wanted as I wasn't specific enough. I realized that I had to include the words _software/hard_ into my searches. For software, I found websites such as [Praxisemr](https://www.praxisemr.com/), which helps doctors manage patient records, prescriptions, and medical visits, and appointments, [Symptomdx.](https://www.symptomdx.com/), providing information about symptoms, possible medical conditions, as well as treatments, and [VisualDx](https://www.visualdx.com/), which provides medical information and pictures to help identify diseases. For hardware, I found websites like [EKO](https://www.ekohealth.com/), a stethoscope that provides digital information, amplifies body sounds, displays heart rates, and ECG information, [Optomed](https://www.optomed.com/us/auroraaeye), a portable camera using Smart Autofocus and Auto Exposure features to take detailed photos for diabetic retinopathy,[Ge HealthCare](https://www.gehealthcare.com/en-us/products/ultrasound/handheld-ultrasound), a wireless and handheld ultrasound device that could make informed treatment decisions and is used in examinations like abdominals, cardiac, along with bladder. I also discovered [VoxScan](https://voxscan.com/) which is a hardware and turns CT and MRI scans into interactive 3D visuals that count be recorded and shared.

After I researched technologies in medical practice, I organized them into categories based on the information they provided. These categories were **written information, sound information, visual information, and 3D information**. The technologies that provided written information, [Praxisemr](https://www.praxisemr.com/),  [Symptomdx.](https://www.symptomdx.com/), and [VisualDx](https://www.visualdx.com/) as they gave provided patient records, symptoms, treatments, and diseases. The technology that provided sound information is [EKO](https://www.ekohealth.com/) as sounds from inside the body could be listened to and recorded. For image information, I grouped [Optomed](https://www.optomed.com/us/auroraaeye) and [Ge HealthCare](https://www.gehealthcare.com/en-us/products/ultrasound/handheld-ultrasound) because both of these technologies provided images of different parts of the body. Lastly, I put [VoxScan](https://voxscan.com/) under the category of 3D information as it turned CT and MRI scans into 3D interactive visuals, allowing users to zoom in, rotate, and explore the 3D structures. Organizing the technologies in these categories made it easier to understand how they are used in medical practice.
